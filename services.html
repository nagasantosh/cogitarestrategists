OUR SERVICES — IN DETAIL

What each engagement actually gives you.

01
DISCOVER

Identifying Agentic AI Opportunities

TYPICALLY 1–2 WEEKS
WHAT IT IS

A structured discovery exercise that surfaces and prioritises the AI opportunities specific to your business and sector.

THE VALUE YOU GET

Clarity on where AI can create real value — ranked by ROI and feasibility — so effort goes only where it pays off.

RECOMMENDED FOR

Leaders exploring AI but unsure where to start, or wanting to cut through the hype.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A prioritised shortlist of opportunities with estimated impact, effort, and quick wins.

02
ASSESS

Assessment for AI Implementation Readiness

TYPICALLY 2–3 WEEKS
WHAT IT IS

An honest health-check of your data, technology, team skills, and processes against what AI actually requires.

THE VALUE YOU GET

Avoid costly false starts by knowing your gaps — and how to close them — upfront. Includes DPDP Act and data-privacy readiness.

RECOMMENDED FOR

Organisations preparing to commit budget to AI and wanting to de-risk the decision.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A readiness scorecard, gap analysis, and prioritised recommendations.

03
PLAN

Future AI Roadmap

TYPICALLY 2–4 WEEKS
WHAT IT IS

A practical, sequenced AI plan aligned to your business goals, budget, and priorities.

THE VALUE YOU GET

A board-ready roadmap setting out what to do now, next, and later — sequencing spend so you see returns before committing further.

RECOMMENDED FOR

Decision-makers who need a defensible plan to align stakeholders and budgets.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A phased roadmap with priorities, indicative costs and effort, and clear milestones.

04
PROVE

Pilot Design, Build & Evaluation

TYPICALLY 4–8 WEEKS
WHAT IT IS

A low-risk, time-boxed pilot built around success metrics agreed with you upfront.

THE VALUE YOU GET

Real evidence of value — or an honest “not yet” — before any large investment, with an objective go / no-go recommendation.

RECOMMENDED FOR

Teams who want proof, not promises, before they scale.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A working pilot, measured results against targets, and a scale-or-stop recommendation.

05
BUILD

Custom Tools, Workflows & Agent Systems

SCOPED PER PROJECT
WHAT IT IS

Tailored AI tools, automated workflows, and agent systems, built and integrated into your existing setup.

THE VALUE YOU GET

Solutions that fit your processes and systems, cut manual effort and cost, and scale as you grow — with the IP and code owned by you.

RECOMMENDED FOR

Organisations ready to move from pilot to real, deployed systems.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Production-ready solutions, integrated and documented, with security and ongoing support.
