The question

What happens when AI becomes part of the team?

Most organizations are still evaluating AI as a tool. A more important question is beginning to emerge.

When AI becomes capable of supporting decisions, retaining knowledge, improving workflows and contributing to outcomes, how should organizations respond?

For leaders, this changes the conversation from: “Which AI tool should we use?” to “How should our organization work with AI?”
